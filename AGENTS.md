[go-workspace/docs/agents/](https://github.com/orglang/go-workspace/tree/main/docs/agents/).

OrgLang project.

At minimum, read:

- [Git and PR Safety](https://github.com/orglang/go-workspace/blob/main/docs/agents/git.md)
